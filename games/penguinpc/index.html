🎮 HOW TO PLAY - BEGINNER'S GUIDE

Welcome to PC Building Empire! Build PCs, install Linux OS, fulfill orders, and grow your computer empire!
STEP 1 📦

Buy Parts

Walk up to the Parts Store counter at top-left to purchase component boxes ($1,200 each).

STEP 2 🔧

Assemble PC

Carry parts to a Build Desk ([BUILD #1]). Press and hold E / SPACE to assemble the rig.

STEP 3 🖥️

Test & Install OS

Carry the assembled PC to your Testing Desk ([TEST #1]). Hold E / SPACE to boot & install Linux!

STEP 4 💵

Sell & Dispatch

Take the tested PC to the Admin Terminal to list it, then drop the boxed PC off at the Dispatch Bay!

🐧
Staff Automation: Open the Staff menu to hire Purchaser, Builder, Tester, Seller, and Dispatcher penguins to run your facility automatically!
📞
Support Calls: Gated until you expand to 5 desks! At 5 desks, you get a gifted Helpdesk to handle incoming support calls.
