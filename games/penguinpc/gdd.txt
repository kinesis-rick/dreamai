PC Building Empire
Game Design Document (GDD)

1. Executive Summary & Vision
●
●
●

Title / Working Name: PC Building Empire (Tux's Rig Empire)
Genre: 2D Isometric Simulation / Time Management / "Spinning Plates" Tycoon
Target Platform: HTML5 (Browser-native via WebGL / Phaser 3), Desktop packaging
via Tauri / Electron

●

Visual Style: Cute 2D isometric pixel art (similar to Stardew Valley / Overcooked),
featuring animated penguin characters

●

Core Philosophy: A tactile, multi-tasking management loop where manual actions
gradually transition into automated logistics, periodically disrupted by high-stakes
customer support crises.

2.2 Co-op Interaction Rules
●

Station Mutex: Each desk or terminal permits only one penguin at a time to prevent
race conditions.

●

Item Passing: Finished boxed PCs can be picked up, dropped, and transferred
between players before reaching the dispatch zone.

●

Shared Economy: Players share a common cash balance, debt liability, and company
star rating.

3. Core Micro-Loop (Single PC
Lifecycle)
Every computer follows a strict 8-step finite state machine:

1. Buy Parts: Player interacts with desk ($1,200 component cost, 1s active click).
2. Assemble Hardware: Player holds action key at the desk (4s active hold).
3. Install Linux OS: Boot PC and trigger installation (5s passive progress bar; player is free
to leave the desk).

4. List for Sale: Turn off PC, interact with the Admin Terminal (1s active click).
5. Buyer Matching: Background sales algorithm finds a buyer (10s passive countdown).
6. Box & Label: Player packs the sold PC into a shipping box and applies a label (3s active
hold).

7. Carry to Dispatch: Player carries the box to the shipping placement bay (~2s travel
time).

8. Delivery Collection: Delivery van arrives, loads the PC, and credits cash (15s passive
timer).

●
●
●

Total Elapsed Time per PC: ~41 seconds
Active Hands-On Time: ~11 seconds
Design Purpose: Passive timers create natural windows for players to "spin plates"
across multiple desks.

6. Automation: Penguin Helpers
Unlocked at Desk 8. Each helper automates a specific task in the production pipeline.

6.1 Roles & Wages (120-Second Day Cycle)
●
●
●
●

Buyer Penguin: Hire: $2,000 | Wage: $150/day — Orders parts for idle desks.
Builder Penguin: Hire: $3,500 | Wage: $250/day — Assembles hardware on desks.
Installer Penguin: Hire: $2,800 | Wage: $200/day — Boots and installs Linux OS.
Seller Penguin: Hire: $2,500 | Wage: $180/day — Lists ready PCs at the Admin
Terminal.

●

Dispatcher Penguin: Hire: $3,200 | Wage: $220/day — Boxes, labels, and carries PCs
to dispatch bay.

●

Support Tech Penguin: Hire: $5,500 | Wage: $400/day — Automatically answers and
resolves Helpdesk tickets.

6.2 Payroll & Bankruptcy Risk
Penguin wages are deducted automatically at the end of each 120-second day cycle. Hiring
excessive workers without matching desk throughput drains cash and leads to insolvency.

7. The Helpdesk System & Review
Ratings
Unlocked at 10 desks. Phone calls and ticket beeps introduce operational friction.

7.1 Mechanics
●
●

Ticket Trigger: Random incoming ticket alert with a 10-second countdown to answer.
Frequency: 1 ticket every 75–90s base, scaling with desk count (+1 ticket frequency
tier per 10 desks).

●

Resolution Lock: Requires 20 seconds of continuous progress bar interaction.

7.2 Rating & Penalty Matrix
●
●
●
●
●

≤ 6s to answer: 5 Stars -> +$200 cash bonus
7s to answer: 4 Stars -> +$100 cash bonus
8s to answer: 3 Stars -> +$40 cash bonus
9s to answer: 2 Stars -> $0 bonus
10s (Missed): 1 Star -> -10% sale price penalty on next 3 PCs sold

○
○
○

Sale price drops from $1,320 to $1,188 (-$132).
Since parts cost $1,200, the player loses -$12 per PC on those 3 sales.
Penalties stack: multiple missed calls compound losses, creating a potential
bankruptcy spiral.

8. Technical Architecture (HTML5 &
WebGL)
●

Rendering: WebGL 2D Isometric projection using sprite sheets and dynamic Y-sorting
(screenY = (x + y) * (tileHeight / 2)).

●

AI Pathfinding: EasyStar.js / A* grid navigation with collision masking around active
desks.

●

Input Management: Unified input merger reading keyboard state map and
navigator.getGamepads().

●

State & Economy: Decoupled fixed-rate simulation tick (20 Hz) with local storage
persistence (localStorage / IndexedDB).

●

Optimization: Viewport culling and sprite batching to maintain 60 FPS up to 200 desks
and dozens of penguins.

11. Penguin Animation System & Directional Perspectives
11.1 Isometric View Perspectives
To match the 2:1 isometric projection of the room, characters navigate across four primary
isometric diagonals:

●

South-West (SW / Down-Left): Front 3/4 perspective facing toward the bottom-left of
the screen. Shows facial expressions, beak, eyes, and white chest belly.

●
●

South-East (SE / Down-Right): Front 3/4 perspective facing toward the bottom-right.
North-West (NW / Up-Left): Back 3/4 perspective facing away from the camera
toward the top-left. Features back plumage, silhouette of beak, and rear foot lift.

●

North-East (NE / Up-Right): Back 3/4 perspective facing away toward the top-right.

11.2 Animation Cycles & Frame Timing
●

Base Frame Rate: 6.6 FPS (150 ms per frame) for walking, producing an exaggerated,
cute penguin waddle.

●

Idle Cycle (2 Frames, 400 ms/frame): Subtle breathing motion and resting flipper
position.

●

Walk Cycle (4 Frames per Direction):

○

Frame 0 (Left Foot Plant): Left webbed foot steps forward, right foot trailing,
body tilts slightly.

○

Frame 1 (Passing / Mid-Waddle): Weight transfers, body bobs upward by 1–2
pixels, flippers counter-balance.

○

Frame 2 (Right Foot Plant): Right webbed foot steps forward, left foot trailing,
opposite body lean.

○
●

Frame 3 (Passing / Mid-Waddle): Weight transfers back with upward bob.

Interacting / Assembling Cycle (2 Frames, 200 ms/frame): Rapid flipper tapping and
tool motion used during desk assembly, Linux installation, and admin listing.

●

Carrying Box Walk Cycle (4 Directions): Penguin holds a sealed cardboard shipping
box in both flippers at chest height while executing the 4-direction walk cycle to the
dispatch placement bay.
