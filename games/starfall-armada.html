How to Play
- Enemies arrive in formations; they try to stay in formation while moving as a group and occasionally fire at you.
- Collect power-ups to boost your weapon's damage, rate of fire, spread, and directional shots.
- Between waves, spend credits in the Shop to improve your ship's shields, maneuverability, and weapons.
- The boss on Wave 5 has heavy shields and tells: watch the swoop, shoot, and smash patterns; strike during openings.
- Backgrounds change per wave with a tech space theme.
