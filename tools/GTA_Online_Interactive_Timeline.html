🏛 Evolution of the GTA Online Criminal Empire (2013–2026)

How Rockstar Games evolved player enterprise and criminal progression over 14 years:

Phase 1: Humble Street Beginnings (2013-2014)

Player property was strictly capped at 1 apartment. Cash was earned primarily through Gerald and Simeon contact missions, street races, and robbing liquor stores.

Phase 2: Cooperative Heists (2015)

High-end apartment planning boards activated. 4-player coordinated operations introduced massive multi-million dollar payouts and the $10M Criminal Mastermind challenge.

Phase 3: The CEO & Contraband Revolution (2015-2016)

SecuroServ VIP and CEO empires. High-rise skyscraper offices, Special Cargo and Vehicle Cargo warehouses, introducing permanent high-risk public lobby sell missions.

Phase 4: Outlaw Biker Networks (2016)

Motorcycle Clubhouses and the Open Road darknet. Sourcing supplies to run illicit manufacturing: Cocaine Lockups, Meth Labs, Counterfeit Cash, Weed Farms, and Document Forgery.

Phase 5: Military Hardware & Doomsday (2017)

Subterranean missile Bunkers, Mobile Operations Centers (MOC), weaponized vehicles, research for explosive ammo, and high-tech subterranean Facilities with Orbital Cannons.

Phase 6: Nightclubs & Logistics Automation (2018)

Gay Tony Prince returns. Players buy luxury Nightclubs that conceal 5 underground warehouse levels where automated technicians passively siphon product from all owned businesses.

Phase 7: The Diamond Casino & Master Terminal (2019)

Table games, Master Penthouses, Retro Arcades, and the Master Control Terminal (MCT) enabling remote management of every business across the map, plus the 3-approach Casino Heist.

Phase 8: Cayo Perico & Full Solo Freedom (2020)

The first external landmass. Infiltrating El Rubio's private island via the nuclear submarine Kosatka with Pavel, establishing the first 100% solo-playable major heist.

Phase 9: Car Culture & Celebrity Fixers (2021-2022)

LS Car Meet, Auto Shop robbery contracts, Franklin Clinton's celebrity solutions Agency, Dr. Dre's unreleased music, and Imani Tech missile lock-on jammers.

Phase 10: Private Sessions, Law & Mansions (2022-2026)

Allowing all sales in Invite-Only sessions, Salvage Yards with Yusuf Amir, purchasable Police Cruisers, Bottom Dollar Bail Enforcement, Money Fronts laundering, and Vinewood Hills Mansions.
